Plantation Activities

Pruning
Trimming the coffee bushes to remove dead branches and promote healthy, productive growth.

Harvesting
Carefully hand-picking only the ripest red coffee cherries from the branches.

Drying
Spreading the harvested beans under the sun on raised beds to lower their moisture content perfectly.
